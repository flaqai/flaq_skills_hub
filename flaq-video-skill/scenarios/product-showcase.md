# Product showcase

Turn an existing product image or person-with-product composition into a demonstration video. Use this for a product detail shot, holding a product toward the camera, or showing a simple use action. For a full ad narrative, use [UGC ads](ugc-ads.md).

## Ask your agent

> Use Flaq Video Skill to animate this photo of a person holding my product. Have them gently present it toward the camera. Keep the product shape, label, color, and the person's identity consistent. Check supported options and cost first.

## Inputs and workflow

- Required: the product image or a composed image showing the person and product.
- Useful details: the intended action, important details to preserve, framing, duration, and audio preference.

1. Inspect whether the requested action is possible from the supplied composition. A product-only image does not provide the identity of a human presenter. If identity matters, request a composed image or suitable references and verify model support.
2. Follow the shared priority to find an image-to-video or reference-guided operation. Read its detailed Flaq Docs for the supported number and roles of images; do not copy input limits advertised on the source websites.
3. Choose one clear action, such as lifting a bottle or presenting a lipstick. Describe camera and product motion separately. Minimize rapid rotations when label fidelity matters.
4. Prepare documented input fields and prompt, submit within the authorized scope, and track the task. If result inspection is available, check hand contact, object shape, labels, and movement.
