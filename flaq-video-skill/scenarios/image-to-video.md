# Image to video

Animate a supplied image into a scene with subject motion, environmental motion, or a camera move. Use this general guide when the request is not better covered by outfit, product, or UGC workflows.

## Ask your agent

> Use Flaq Video Skill to animate my attached landscape image. Add a slow camera push-in and gentle movement in the trees while preserving the composition and lighting. Recommend the latest suitable Seedance option and check duration, framing, and cost first.

## Inputs and workflow

- Required: a starting image and the desired action or camera movement.
- Useful details: duration, aspect ratio, style, audio, and whether the ending must match a particular image.

1. Discover an image-to-video operation and verify the input role in its exact Flaq Docs. A starting-frame input, reference image, and ending-frame input are different capabilities.
2. Separate what must stay fixed from what should move. For a subtle animation, preserve the source composition; for a new scene, clarify how far the scene may depart from the image.
3. Match the amount of action to supported duration. If an exact final frame is required, verify ending-frame support rather than assuming a prompt can guarantee it.
4. Construct and submit the documented request with the user's image, track its task ID, and deliver. If the host can inspect it, check continuity and whether the action reaches the intended ending.

```text
Cinematic 9:16 summer travel vlog shot on a handheld iPhone 15 Pro with authentic lived-in realism, natural sun-drenched lighting, and real skin texture with visible pores. Subtle handheld micro-shakes, natural autofocus hunting, and minor exposure shifts in direct sunlight. Absolutely no background music; layered diegetic foley only. 0-2s [Arrival / Room]: Front wide-angle handheld selfie camera slowly pushing in. A beautiful young blonde Caucasian woman in a loose white linen shirt and shorts rolls her suitcase into a bright luxury resort suite with white linen decor and warm afternoon sunlight. She smiles brightly into the lens and speaks with clear lip-sync: "Finally, summer vacation starts!"

2-4s [Balcony Reveal]: Close-up of her hand pulling open sheer white curtains, followed by an immediate rack focus to the outdoor resort waterpark below the balcony, clearly showing a teal splash pool and a massive open turquoise waterslide. Quick cut to her medium close-up reaction shot: eyes widening in delight, an excited and eager smile spreading across her face.

4-6s [Waterpark Pathway]: Costume changes instantly to a minimalist black bikini, barefoot, completely dry blonde hair, no sunglasses or accessories. Handheld tracking camera follows her as she briskly walks down the wet stone resort path toward the exact same open waterslide seen from the balcony. Shimmering pools, sun umbrellas, and tropical foliage pass in the background.

6-8s [Slide Entrance Montage]: Quick dynamic sequence: extreme close-up of bare feet stepping onto wet stairs -> tilt up along the safety railings -> top slide deck. She sits into the mouth of the massive open waterslide in proper water-sliding posture with legs extended forward and hands braced on the sides. Front close-up of her thrilled expression as she looks at the camera and says with clear lip-sync: "Here I go!"

8-11s [High-Speed Slide Action]: Rigid front-facing waterproof wide-angle camera locked onto her face and upper body. High-speed descent down the open flume, body leaning naturally through two curved banks. Wind whips her dry blonde hair; authentic ecstatic laughter and gasps as sunlight, shadows, and water spray streak past. She stays firmly seated in the flume at all times.

11-13s [Pool Splashdown]: Low-angle waterproof camera stationed just above the surface of the splash pool at the slide exit. She shoots out at high speed into the pool, creating a massive, photorealistic splash that engulfs the lens. Real water droplets hit and stick to the lens surface. She is briefly submerged beneath the water.

13-15s [Out-of-Water Ending]: She emerges gracefully from the pool with completely soaking wet hair slicked back. Three rapid cuts: (1) Front close-up: she sweeps her wet hair back with both hands as water streams down her face and bare shoulders; (2) Profile close-up: wet strands cling to her cheeks and neck as water drips; (3) Front close-up: she turns back to the camera and bursts into an authentic, beaming laugh, freezing on the final frame. Water droplets remain visibly clinging to the camera lens.

Audio: Realistic Foley throughout: rolling suitcase wheels, sliding curtain fabric, distant resort pool splashing, summer cicadas, wet bare footsteps on stone, stairs climbing, roaring water inside the flume, wind rush, deep heavy pool splash, and genuine emerging laughter.
```
